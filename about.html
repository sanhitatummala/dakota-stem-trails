About Us

What are we?

Dakota STEM Trails, housed in Brookings, South Dakota, is a student-led organization working to make STEM education more accessible to children in rural communities. Through hands-on activities, creative challenges, workshops, and community partnerships, we bring meaningful STEM experiences directly to students and help them discover their potential as future scientists, engineers, innovators, and problem-solvers.
