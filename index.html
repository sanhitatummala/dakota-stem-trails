Dakota STEM Trails

Dakota STEM Trails is a student-led organization that inspires children in rural communities to explore STEM through accessible, creative, hands-on learning opportunities and meaningful community partnerships.

Our Mission

To inspire and encourage children across rural South Dakota to explore science, technology, engineering, and mathematics through accessible, creative, and community-centered learning opportunities

Core Principles

Student-Led

High-school students plan programs, lead activities, and make organizational decisions.

Locally relevant

Activities connect STEM to agriculture, weather, wildlife, water, energy, and rural community life.

Accessible

Programs use affordable materials and can travel to schools, libraries, and community centers.

Hands-on

Children learn by asking questions, testing ideas, building models, and solving problems.

Collaborative

Community partners provide mentorship, expertise, venues, volunteers, and resources.
